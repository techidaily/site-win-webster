## Join the Gala! Support Night To Shine with PCDJ & Party Tyme's Exclusive Event

Free Chapel of of Georgia reached out to us a couple weeks ago in a bind. They wanted to setup a karaoke system for their upcoming “Night to Shine” charity event but their budget for entertainment was tight. After a trip to google, their entertainment director found our new [LYRX karaoke software](http://www.lyrxkaraoke.com/) online and read that the [Party Tyme Karaoke Subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) was integrated — it sounded like the perfect solution, but they simply didn’t have the time of funding to make things happen quickly.

We teamed up with Party Tyme to make sure the “Night to Shine” event, hosted by Tim Tebow (The Tim Tebow Foundation) of Florida Gators and Denver Broncos fame, was well equipped for those that wanted to croon.

Hundreds of people attended and karaoke was a huge success.

> _“Thank you for your help with the Night To Shine event. The Karaoke was the highlight of the evening for so many people. All of our guests felt like stars! LYRX is so user-friendly. The process went smoothly from set up to tear down. The song selection from Party Tyme covered every song request from “Good Good Father” to “Sweet Home Alabama”. I would highly recommend your services to anyone looking to spice up their event.”_ **Chris Smith – Entertainment Director**
