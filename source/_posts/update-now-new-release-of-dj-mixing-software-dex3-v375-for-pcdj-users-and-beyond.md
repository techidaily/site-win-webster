In previous DEX 3 iterations it was necessary to utilize the **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** website to build and save playlists for offline use – that’s no longer the case, DJs can download and/or remove tracks from their local hard drive without every leaving the browser. New icons for ‘track state’ are displayed to the right of track titles for all **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** tracks that indicate whether the track is stored in the cloud (cloud icon), being downloaded (downward arrow) or stored on your hard drive (check mark).

### What’s New, Changed And Updated In Version 3.7.5

* Contextual menu to add **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** songs to the Locker (to “Offline tracks”); you can also remove them as well as download as many track selections at a time as you wish.
* Right-click on Pulselocker search results and use the “_Download To Offline Tracks_” option to retrieve songs to your hard drive. Use “_Remove From Offline Tracks_” to dump.
* Added icons to show the status of a **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** track (downloading, downloaded, removing, cloud, etc)
* Updated **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** SDK to latest version (1.2.2)
* Excluded **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** cloud tracks from the regular search results (it will still show tracks from the Locker – tracks that you have on your computer)
* Turn off **[Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** via checkbox in options
* 7 new [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) supported (zero-configure / map files included with software installation):  
– Behringer CMD Studio 2A  
– Behringer CMD Studio 4A  
– Hercules DJControl Instinct P8  
– Reloop DJ Beatmix 4 MK2  
– Pioneer DJ DDJ-RB  
– Pioneer DJ DDJ-RR  
– Pioneer DDJ WeGO2 (the original WeGO and the WeGO3 are also supported)
* Updated M-Audio Xponent script (auto detection should work OK in all instances)
* Other minor bug fixes and performance optimizations (eg. slightly faster searching/search results)
