## Unlock Audience Secrets with Top DJs: Dave Winsor and Jason Jones Host 'Thursday Mobile Beats' - Don't Miss Out on #DJNTV Insights!

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/knowingyouraudience.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/knowingyouraudience.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "knowingyouraudience")

DJNTV’s (_Disc Jockey News TV_) Mobile Music Thursday with DJ Jason Jones features mobile DJs from around the country who are playing weddings, schools and bars to find out how and what they play and when they play it. This week we feature Dave Winsor, Disc Jockey News writer and owner of [Winsor Entertainment](http://winsorweddings.com/) and morning show host on [99.9 The Wolf](http://wtht.nh1media.com/)in Portland Maine.

In this segment Dave Winsor explains how to ask your client the right questions to figure out what song selections and genres you’ll want to base your event set around, including “_hairbrush songs_“.

Dave takes queues from “_cocktail hour_” to get a feel for what the nights vibe will be like, and applies that to his song selection. Knowing your audience is paramount and Dave provides tips on how to properly read the crowd, and takes musical risks other DJs wouldn’t consider – with a great deal of success.
