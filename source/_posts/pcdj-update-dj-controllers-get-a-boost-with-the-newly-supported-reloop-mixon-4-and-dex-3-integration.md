## PCDJ Update: DJ Controllers Get a Boost with the Newly Supported REloop MIXON 4 and DEX 3 Integration

[![Reloop Mixon 4 DJ controller map for DEX 3](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/mixon4-cover.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/mixon4-cover.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Reloop Mixon 4 DJ controller map for DEX 3")

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Reloop DJ Mixon 4 DJ controller front](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/reloop-mixon-4-front-top.jpg?fit=300%2C130&ssl=1 "Reloop DJ Mixon 4 DJ controller front")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/reloop-mixon-4-front-top.jpg?fit=1030%2C445&ssl=1)

The **Mixon 4 DJ controller** from up-and-coming brand [**Reloop DJ**](http://www.reloop.com/reloop-mixon-4) is their flagship model, showcasing 4-deck mixing and hybrid cross-platform support for MAC, Windows, iOS and Android (with tablet slot). With the downloaded map below, which will be included along with the [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) installer in all future versions beginning with the soon to launch 3.8, [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) users have plug-and-play support with 4-deck control.

The **Reloop Mixon 4** is a serious bit of kit with a layout and feature set similar, and in a few ways beyond, what you find included on higher end pro-ready [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) on the market. Selling for $799.99 USD on most DJ equipment websites it’s also reasonably priced with it’s vast array of controls.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/reloop-mixon-4-hybrid-dj-controller-front-back.jpg?fit=300%2C120&ssl=1 "Reloop DJ Mixon 4 DJ controller front and back")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/reloop-mixon-4-hybrid-dj-controller-front-back.jpg?fit=630%2C253&ssl=1)

Featured on this large chassis DJ controller are 16 Color-Coded RGB Drum Pads for your finger drumming delight, Slip and Loop Modes, Large Aluminum Jog Wheels with LEDs, FX controls, Integrated USB 2.0 ASIO/Core Audio Interface and unique controls for harmonic/key mixing (support for the harmonic mixing features will be improved in DEX 3.8 and beyond).

For DJs looking for a high-end professional grade controller that supports 4-deck mixing with DEX 3 the **Reloop Mixon 4** is a great choice for under 1K. As noted, improved support for the Reloop Mixon 4 will be available in the next public release of DEX 3, which is set to launch next week.

Download the map for the **Reloop Mixon 4** below, install as explained and launch [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and you’re ready to mix music and video!

[Download Reloop Mixon 4 Map For DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

**INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:** With DEX 3 closed – Unzip the Map (js file only) to: **Documents -> PCDJ-DEX3 -> Controllers**

**NOTE:** If you already have DEX 3.8 or higher, the MAP is already pre-installed!

### Reloop Mixon 4 Features & Specifications

* 4-Channel flagship controller
* Industry first hybrid design
* Multi-Platform support for iOS/Android/PC/Mac
* First, integrated docking station that holds up to an iPad PRO (12.9”)
* Large 1.2 x 1.2″ drum pads with multi-colored backlit LEDs and velocity sensitive technology
* 8 color-coded performance modes including Pitch Play
* Slicer performance modes for cutting and mashing up your tracks
* Up to eight Hot Cues and Cue Loops can be triggered on each deck
* Access and fire up to eight saved Loops and Loop Rolls
* Mighty SP-6 sample bank control with dedicated volume
* Slip mode for creative live remixing
* Dedicated Sound Color filter (LPF/HPF) on each channel
* Macro FX Mode: Combine any effect with filters on each channel
* Dedicated Loop Control Section with Length Indication LED-Bar
* Large (6.1″ / 155mm) aluminum jog wheels with 2-part touch-sensitive technology
* Visual feedback on Jog Wheels through LED illumination for Virtual Needle position & Track Time Elapsed
* Harmonic mixing with Key Sync, Key Shift and Pitch Play
* Hi-Res 14-bit pitch fader with range and Key Lock control
* Mix millions of tracks instantly with award-winning djay 2 and Spotify integration
* Large transport section
* Independent Level meters for In- and Outputs
* Fader-start feature
* Quick-Skip: Fast search within a track
* Sturdy metal-panel construction with performance faders & knobs in club size and quality
* Extreme tight vinyl emulation for superior scratching experience
* Control DJ effects (FX engine by iZotope / Sugar Bytes) on two independent effect units
* Crossfader curve & assignment controls
* Shift buttons for second layer control
* Anti-Theft: Kensington lock slot
* ASIO drivers (CORE compliant) with minimal latency and high audio quality (PC/Mac)
* MIDI-Ready: Compatible with every DJ software; Mappings available for DEX 3 above
* Master Output: Stereo XLR and Stereo RCA
* Booth Output: Stereo 1/4″
* Mic Input: One 1/4″ with volume control knob
* Headphone Output: 1x 1/4″ and 1x 1/8″
* Integrated 4-channel USB audio club soundcard with High Retention connection port
* USB-, Lightning-, Android-, OTG-cable and external power supply with grounding included
